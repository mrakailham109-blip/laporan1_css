03

THE
PROCESS

Every frame is intentional. Here's how a shot comes to life — from concept to final export.

01

OBSERVE

Before the shutter clicks, I study the scene. Light direction, subject movement, background noise. Patience is the first tool.

02

COMPOSE

Rule of thirds, leading lines, negative space. Composition is not a formula — it's a controlled instinct trained over time.

03

CAPTURE

Shoot in RAW. Expose for the highlights. The decisive moment exists only once — be ready when it arrives.

04

REFINE

Post-processing is the darkroom of the digital age. Subtle grading, not heavy filters. The photo speaks; editing just clears its throat.
